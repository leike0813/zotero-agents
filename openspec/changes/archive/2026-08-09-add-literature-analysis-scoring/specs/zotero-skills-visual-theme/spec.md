## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rating stars SHALL share one cross-surface visual language

Rating renderers SHALL use one score-to-star model and equivalent accessible
states across Zotero and browser documents.

#### Scenario: Rating is rendered in either supported surface

- **WHEN** the Zotero library or Synthesis Index renders a score
- **THEN** it SHALL use the same full, half, empty, and missing star model
- **AND** colors SHALL remain legible in light, dark, and selected-row states
- **AND** the star group SHALL expose one localized accessible label.
